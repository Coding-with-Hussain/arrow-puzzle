Arrow Escape – Free Online Arrow Puzzle Game

Arrow Escape is a free logic puzzle you can play right in your browser. Every board is filled with arrows, and each one can only slide the way it points. Tap an arrow to send it off the board — but only if nothing stands in its path. Clear every arrow to escape the level.

The first boards take seconds. Later ones hide long chains of dependencies: one arrow blocks another, which blocks three more. Spotting which arrow can move first — without bumping into anything — is where the real puzzle lies.

How to Play

  1. Tap or click an arrow to slide it off the board.
  2. Arrows only travel the way they point: up, down, left or right.
  3. If another arrow is anywhere in its path, it’s blocked. Bumping a blocked arrow costs a move.
  4. Clear every arrow to finish the level. Fewer moves earn more stars.

Game Features

  • 100 levels, from gentle 3×3 boards to tricky 8×8 expert puzzles.
  • Every level is checked by a solver, so every board can be cleared.
  • Undo, restart and hints whenever you need them.
  • Stars and best scores for every level, saved automatically on your device.
  • Made for touch screens and works just as well with a mouse or keyboard.
  • Keeps working offline once it has loaded. No account, no sign-up, no download.

Tips & Tricks

  • Trace the whole lane: an arrow is blocked by anything between it and the edge, not just its neighbour.
  • Work backwards: if the arrow you want is stuck, free whatever is blocking it first.
  • Look for arrows with an empty lane to the edge — they are always safe to tap.
  • Save hints for when you are truly stuck. Each one costs a move.

FAQ

Is Arrow Escape free?

Yes. All 100 levels are free to play in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your progress is stored in your browser on this device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Arrow Escape is designed for touch screens first and works on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Can I play offline?

Yes. After your first visit the game is saved by your browser, so you can keep playing without a connection.

Can every level be solved?

Always. Every level is verified by a solver before it ships, so there is always a way to clear the board.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress saves automatically. Clearing your browser data, or playing in a private window, starts you fresh.
